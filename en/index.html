01 / Instituto Semear · Jun 2026 - Jul 2026

People Analytics System for Selection Process

Automated Analysis and Diversity Indicators

Problem
No way to evaluate the selection process as a whole: data from 5 editions scattered across spreadsheets and forms, with no view of conversion, bias or evaluation consistency.
Result
12,000+ applications from 5 editions analyzed in one place
  • Python
  • Pandas
  • Streamlit
  • YData Profiling
  • SQL
people-analytics · streamlit
  1. 01Data pipeline with automated cleaning, deduplication and consolidation of 5 editions, with built-in quality checks that flagged 928 duplicate submissions
  2. 02Funnel analysis across every stage (application → group dynamic → interview → approval), finding the main bottleneck: only 22.9% of applicants reached the group dynamic
  3. 03Fairness metrics: a representativeness index comparing approved vs. applicant profiles across demographic groups
  4. 04Evaluation consistency: score distributions per criterion, correlation between stages and outlier detection across 75 evaluators
  5. 05Auto-generated executive summary with confidence labels on each insight
  1. Forms & spreadsheets
  2. Clean & deduplicate
  3. Funnel & fairness metrics
